Part 2 Script
Good afternoon. I am Sergeant Ellis, and I want to explain several changes to our community-safety programme. First, the evening patrol route now includes the new shopping area because reports there have increased since it opened. [Q11: A] If you notice suspicious behaviour, do not confront anyone and do not post names online. The first step is to call the non-emergency police number. [Q12: B] At the weekend market, phones are the item reported missing most frequently, more often than wallets or bicycles. [Q13: C] Cyclists should attach a visible identification sticker to the frame; a strong lock is still important, but the sticker helps us return recovered bikes. [Q14: A] Our Saturday safety desk operates from nine in the morning until one in the afternoon. [Q15: B]
Now for the free services. Property marking takes place at the Community Centre. [Q16: E] The lost-property desk is beside the market entrance. [Q17: C] Bicycle safety checks are carried out in the Riverside car park. [Q18: D] Free personal alarms can be collected from the Town Hall foyer. [Q19: B] Finally, printed neighbourhood maps are available at the Main Library. [Q20: A]
